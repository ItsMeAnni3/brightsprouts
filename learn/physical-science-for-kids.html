What's inside

🧊 Matter & Atoms
Everything around you — this page, the air you breathe, even you — is made of matter. Let's zoom into atoms and see how matter changes state.

Pushes and pulls make the whole world move! Let's explore the forces that start, stop and steer everything around you.
⚡ Energy
Energy makes everything happen — from a bouncing ball to a glowing lightbulb. It never disappears; it just changes from one form to another.
🧲 Electricity & Magnetism
Flip a switch and a room fills with light. Wave a magnet near a paperclip and — snap — it jumps! Electricity and magnetism are secretly best friends.

Key ideas

  • Matter is anything that takes up space and has mass — solid, liquid, gas and plasma are its four states.
  • Newton's three laws explain how forces change motion, from inertia to action-and-reaction pairs.
  • Energy comes in many forms (kinetic, potential, heat, light, sound, electrical) and is never created or destroyed — only changed from one form to another.
  • Electric current needs a complete circuit to flow, and materials are either conductors or insulators.
  • Magnets have north and south poles: like poles repel, opposite poles attract, and a coiled current can even make a temporary electromagnet.

Sample worksheet questions

1. Anything that takes up space and has mass is called ____.
Answer: Matter
2. An object's tendency to keep doing what it's already doing is ____.
Answer: Inertia
3. The rule that energy can't be created or destroyed is the law of ____.
Answer: Conservation of energy
4. Do like magnetic poles attract or repel each other?
Answer: Repel
5. A magnet made using an electric current through a coil of wire is a(n) ____.
Answer: Electromagnet
