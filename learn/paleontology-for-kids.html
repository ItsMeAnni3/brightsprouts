What's inside

🦴 Fossils & Digging Up the Past
Every fossil is a message from the deep past. Paleontologists are scientists who dig them up, piece them together, and read the story they tell about ancient life.
🦖 Dinosaurs
Dinosaurs walked the Earth for over 160 million years — far longer than humans have existed! Let's meet some famous ones and clear up a few myths.
🐚 Prehistoric Life
Not every prehistoric creature was a dinosaur! Long before — and long after — the dinosaurs, other amazing animals ruled the land, sea and sky.
☄️ Extinction & Deep Time
Earth is about 4.6 billion years old, and life's story is written in fossils and rock layers. Sometimes that story includes disaster — huge mass extinctions that changed life forever.

Key ideas

  • A fossil is the preserved remains or trace of a living thing from long ago — most form in sedimentary rock over thousands or millions of years.
  • Dinosaurs lived during the Mesozoic Era, about 252 to 66 million years ago, split into the Triassic, Jurassic and Cretaceous periods.
  • Non-avian dinosaurs died out about 66 million years ago — long before humans existed — but birds are their living descendants.
  • Pterosaurs (flying reptiles) and marine reptiles like plesiosaurs and mosasaurs lived alongside the dinosaurs, but were not dinosaurs themselves.
  • The dinosaur age ended in a mass extinction linked to a huge asteroid impact at Chicxulub, Mexico, about 66 million years ago.

Sample worksheet questions

1. The preserved remains or trace of an ancient living thing is a ____.
Answer: Fossil
2. Dinosaurs lived during which geologic era?
Answer: The Mesozoic Era
3. Were mosasaurs, ichthyosaurs, and plesiosaurs dinosaurs?
Answer: No, they were reptiles
4. The crater left by the dinosaur-ending asteroid is called ____.
Answer: Chicxulub
5. Modern ____ are the living descendants of small meat-eating dinosaurs.
Answer: Birds
