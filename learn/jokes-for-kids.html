Jokes for Kids

100 clean, genuinely funny jokes for grade-schoolers, told by Sprout and his best friend Bud on a little old television. Ten channels, from Animal Planet to Knock-Knock. Every one is safe to tell at school, at the dinner table, or to a grandparent on the phone.

Watch the Joke Show free →

How the Joke Show works

  • Sprout reads the set-up out loud. Try to guess the punchline before you press the button!
  • Press again and the punchline appears — and Bud, who has never once kept a straight face, falls about laughing.
  • Tune to a channel for one kind of joke, or leave it on All Channels for a mixed-up show.
  • Every joke is in the printable Joke Book too, and the How Jokes Work lesson teaches set-ups, punchlines, puns and homophones — so children can write their own.
