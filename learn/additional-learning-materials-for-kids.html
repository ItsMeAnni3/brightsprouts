Why colouring counts as learning

  • Colouring is real learning: it builds the hand muscles and pencil grip a child needs for writing.
  • It trains focus, patience and staying inside a boundary, which is the same control needed to form letters.
  • There are no wrong colours. A purple elephant is a fine choice. Ask "tell me about your picture" rather than "what is it?"
  • For little ones aged three to five, pick the big simple pictures. Older children can add their own backgrounds, patterns and shading.
  • Press the button for a brand new set of pages any time. Every picture is drawn as clean outlines, so crayons, pencils and paint all work.

Try it together

Family Gallery: colour a page each, grown-ups included, then hang them all on the fridge. Ask everyone to explain one colour choice they made. It turns ten quiet minutes into a conversation, and it is the part children remember.
