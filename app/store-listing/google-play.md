## Target audience and content

Select **age groups 5 and under, 6 to 8, and 9 to 12** as well as 13+. Selecting any under-13 group
puts the app in the **Designed for Families** programme, which then requires all of the following,
all of which are already true:

- No ads. (Correct: there are none.)
- No third-party advertising SDKs. (Correct.)
- A privacy policy that covers children. (Section 5 of privacy.html.)
- Content rating completed. (Below.)

**Do not claim the app is for children only** unless you intend to meet the full Families
requirements permanently. "For children and adults" is the honest and simpler selection here.

## Content rating questionnaire

Answer **No** to every question about violence, sexuality, profanity, drugs, gambling, and
user-generated content. The two that need thought:

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Does the app let users interact or exchange content? | **No** | There is no chat with other people, no messaging, no profiles and no way to publish anything. Sprout answers only from the app's own lessons. |
| Does the app share the user's location? | **No** | It never asks for location. |
| Does the app allow purchases? | **Yes** | The $6.99 subscription, taken through Stripe on the website. |

Expected rating: **Everyone / PEGI 3**.

## Data safety section

These answers must match `privacy.html` exactly. Google checks, and a mismatch is a policy strike.

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes.

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Required | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality (replying to a message), and marketing (only if the parent asks for the free packs) |
| Name | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality: only if someone fills in the contact form |
| Phone number | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality: optional field on the contact form |
| Purchase history | Yes | No | Optional | Handled by Stripe for the subscription |
| App activity / page views | Yes | No | Optional | Analytics, via a cookieless counter that does not identify anyone |
| Audio / voice | **No** | **No** | | The microphone is transcribed by the device's browser. The app never receives, records or stores audio. |
| Location, contacts, photos, files, health, financial info | No | No | | Never requested. |

Also declare:

- **Data is encrypted in transit:** Yes (HTTPS everywhere).
- **Users can request that data be deleted:** Yes (by contacting us; most data never leaves the device).
- **Committed to the Play Families Policy:** Yes.
